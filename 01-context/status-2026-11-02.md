## Findings
1. **No directory has a canonical tag** (verified on both reviewed sites: no `rel=canonical` in templates). On uswelldrillers.com every page also returns 200 at `/x`, `/x/` and `www.` variants (Railway static host, no redirects), and internal links and JSON-LD use non-slash URLs while the sitemap uses slash URLs. This is the main cause of the 6,066 duplicates.
2. **gaclosinglawyers.com serves every attorney twice**: `src/pages/[slug].astro` and `src/pages/[county]/[slug].astro` (the nested copy has no schema, malformed HTML and the same title and description). 2,222 of 5,442 sitemap URLs are the nested duplicates, which also breaks the flat-URL rule. Traffic was a June launch blip and has been zero since August. Content depth (about 2,000 characters per profile, scraped firm copy) and the 201-city x 5-template city pages (about 1,000 URLs) are suspected thin or duplicate content; not yet audited.
3. **Large "Discovered / Crawled, not indexed" counts on both sites** point to thin or duplicate programmatic content (R-119). Separate project, not yet scoped.
4. **Automation went quiet**: the last commits in both site repos are newsjacking updates dated 2026-07-07, so the auto-push from the 49ff7bc commit has not produced site commits since. Needs checking.
5. **Repo state**: local `dist/` folders are stale builds (June-July). One dead link was removed (`/claim/<slug>` on uswelldrillers.com contractor pages; no `claim` page exists). The removal is uncommitted in the site repo `C:\Users\tamo4\git\bigfoot-sites\uswelldrillers.com` and is committed by Phase 2 of `p4_canonical`.
6. **Stale control-plane docs**: `03_mechanism_blueprint.bleep.md` listed a single `CURRENT_STATE` of STATE_01 (updated 2026-11-02 to "per directory"), and `04_feedback_loop.bleep.md` lists only R-101 to R-110 while `.system-rules.md` runs to R-131 (not yet reconciled).
